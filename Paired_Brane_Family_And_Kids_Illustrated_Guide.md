### 🫧 Secret #1: Space Is a Double-Decker with Soap-Bubble Bridges!

> 🧪 **2-Minute Kitchen Sink Experiment to Do Together:**
> Mix water + dish soap in a bowl. Take two plastic cookie cutters or rings, dip them in, and slowly pull them apart. Watch the soap film curve into a smooth hourglass waist! Now poke the middle: if you pull the rings too far apart (*pop!*), the bridge snaps back into two flat sheets!

That locked 3-way knot is a **Proton**—the heart of every atom!

> 🖐️ **The 720° Plate Trick (Try This Standing Up!):**
